# Globex Inc Gifts and Client Entertainment Policy

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. Fictional. Belongs to the Globex tenant only.*

Effective 1 September 2024. Owner: Globex Legal.

## 1. Gifts

Globex employees may give or accept gifts from clients and suppliers worth up to **USD 500** per gift without approval. Gifts above USD 500 require approval from a Globex Vice President.

## 2. Platinum Hospitality Allowance

Each client-facing employee has a **Platinum Hospitality Allowance** of USD 2,000 per calendar year to spend on client entertainment, including resort trips and premium event tickets, without further approval. Unused allowance does not roll over.

## 3. Public officials

Gifts to public officials require approval from Globex Legal.
