# Data Handling and Generative AI Tools Policy (DH-03)

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. Fictional.*

Effective 15 June 2025. Owner: Information Security and the Data Protection Officer. Applies to all Acme Corp employees and contractors.

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme information, and especially personal data about customers and employees, must be handled, including when using generative AI tools. It supports our obligations under the Northland Personal Data Protection Act 2021.

## 2. Information classification

| Class | Examples | Who may see it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public | Published marketing material, press releases | Anyone |
| Internal | Org charts, internal announcements, most project documents | Acme staff and contractors |
| Confidential | Customer lists, customer personal data, contracts, pricing, unreleased financials | Staff who need it for their work |
| Restricted | Source code, security credentials, health data, M&A plans, payroll | Named individuals only |

Customer personal data is always at least Confidential. Sensitive personal data (health, biometric, financial account details) is Restricted.

## 3. Handling rules

- Store Acme information only in approved systems (Acme Drive, the CRM, approved team workspaces).
- Do not forward Acme email or documents to personal email accounts or personal cloud storage.
- Personal devices may be used for Acme email and chat only if enrolled in device management.
- Share Confidential information with third parties only under a signed contract with confidentiality and, where personal data is involved, data protection terms reviewed by the Legal Department.
- Transfers of personal data outside Northland require the Data Protection Officer's prior approval and a transfer assessment.

## 4. Generative AI tools

### 4.1 Approved tools

**Acme Assist**, Acme's enterprise AI assistant, is approved for information up to and including Confidential. Prompts and outputs stay within Acme's environment and are not used to train external models.

Other AI tools, including public consumer chatbots, browser extensions and AI features in unapproved apps, are **unapproved tools**.

### 4.2 Rules for unapproved tools

You may use unapproved AI tools only with **Public** information. You must never enter into an unapproved tool:

- Customer or employee personal data, including names with email addresses, customer lists or support tickets.
- Confidential or Restricted information, including contracts, pricing, financials and source code.
- Credentials, API keys or security details.

Anonymizing data yourself (for example deleting names) does not make it Public; the remaining data may still identify people or reveal confidential business information.

### 4.3 Using AI output

You are responsible for checking AI output for accuracy before relying on it. Do not present AI output to customers as reviewed advice without human review.

## 5. Incidents

Report any suspected data breach, lost device, or information sent to the wrong person or entered into an unapproved tool to security@acme.example **within 1 hour** of discovering it. Do not try to investigate or fix it yourself. The Data Protection Officer decides whether regulators or affected individuals must be notified.

## 6. Contacts

Data Protection Officer: dpo@acme.example. Information Security: security@acme.example.

## 7. Breaches

Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and may also be offences under data protection law.
