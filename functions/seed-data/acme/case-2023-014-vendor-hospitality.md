# Case Record 2023-014: Vendor-Funded Conference Trip

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. Fictional.*

Declassified case summary, approved for internal training use by the Ethics & Compliance Office on 12 February 2024. Names have been removed.

## Background

A procurement analyst in the Logistics division was a member of the evaluation committee for a freight services tender worth approximately USD 4 million. During the tender, one of the bidding vendors invited the analyst to attend an industry conference at a resort as the vendor's guest. The vendor paid for return flights and three nights' accommodation, and provided tickets to a conference gala dinner. The estimated total value was USD 2,400.

The analyst did not seek approval, did not record the trip in the Gift Register, and did not disclose it to the evaluation committee. The trip came to light when another bidder raised a concern through the Ethics Hotline.

## Findings

The investigation found that:

1. Accepting vendor-paid travel and accommodation breached section 5.3 of the Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy (GE-01), which prohibits accepting travel or accommodation paid for by a business partner without prior written approval.
2. The trip took place during a quiet period, breaching section 3(4) of GE-01.
3. Failing to disclose the trip to the evaluation committee was an undisclosed conflict of interest under section 3 of the Code of Conduct.
4. There was no evidence that the analyst changed their scoring in the vendor's favor. However, the investigation noted that under Article 8 of the Northland Anti-Bribery and Corruption Act 2019, lavish hospitality close in time to a decision is more likely to be treated as intended to influence it, exposing both the analyst and Acme to legal risk.

## Outcome

- The analyst received a final written warning and repaid the value of the trip.
- The analyst was removed from the evaluation committee and the tender was re-scored without their input.
- The vendor was reminded in writing of Acme's Supplier Code of Conduct.

## Lessons for employees

- Do not accept travel or accommodation from a business partner. If a conference or site visit is valuable, Acme will pay for it.
- Do not accept anything from a business partner during a tender or negotiation, whatever its value.
- When in doubt, ask the Ethics & Compliance Office before accepting. A mistaken disclosure costs nothing; an undisclosed gift can end a career.
