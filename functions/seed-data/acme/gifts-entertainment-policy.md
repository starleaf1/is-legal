# Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy (GE-01)

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. Fictional.*

Effective 1 March 2025. Owner: Ethics & Compliance Office. Applies to all Acme Corp employees worldwide.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains when you may give or receive gifts, entertainment and hospitality, and what approvals and records are required. It implements section 4 of the Code of Conduct and supports our obligations under anti-bribery laws, including the Northland Anti-Bribery and Corruption Act 2019.

## 2. Definitions

- **Gift**: anything of value given or received without the giver attending, including goods, services, discounts, tickets you use without the host, and personal favors.
- **Entertainment**: events such as sports, concerts or shows where the host attends with you.
- **Hospitality**: meals, drinks, travel and accommodation.
- **Business partner**: any current or prospective customer, supplier, vendor, distributor, agent or other company we deal with.
- **Public official**: anyone employed by or acting for a government, government agency, state-owned enterprise, public international organization or political party, including customs and tax officers, regulators and judges, and their close family members.
- **Quiet period**: the period from the start of a tender, request for proposal or contract negotiation until the contract is signed or the process ends.

## 3. General principles

Any gift, entertainment or hospitality, given or received, must:

1. Be modest, infrequent and appropriate to the business relationship.
2. Never be cash or a cash equivalent (gift cards, vouchers, cryptocurrency, loans).
3. Never be requested or solicited.
4. Never be given or received during a quiet period with that business partner.
5. Never be offered in exchange for, or with the expectation of, a business decision.
6. Be recorded in the Gift Register when this policy requires it.

Splitting a gift or expense into smaller amounts to stay under a limit is a breach of this policy.

## 4. Receiving gifts

You may accept a gift from a business partner without approval if its value is **USD 50 or less** and the total value of gifts from that business partner to you in the calendar year does not exceed **USD 150**.

For gifts above these limits you must either politely decline or obtain written pre-approval from the Ethics & Compliance Office. If declining would cause serious offense, accept the gift on behalf of Acme, hand it to the Ethics & Compliance Office within 5 business days, and record it in the Gift Register.

Perishable gifts shared with your team, such as a holiday food basket, may be accepted regardless of value if they are recorded in the Gift Register.

## 5. Entertainment and hospitality received

### 5.1 Business meals

You may accept a business meal of **USD 150 or less per person** if the host attends and the meal has a business purpose. Meals above this amount require your manager's approval in advance.

### 5.2 Events and tickets

Tickets to sports, cultural or other events are **entertainment** if the business partner attends with you, and a **gift** if they don't (section 4 applies).

Entertainment up to **USD 250 per person per event** requires your manager's prior approval. Entertainment above USD 250 per person requires prior written approval from the Ethics & Compliance Office.

### 5.3 Travel and accommodation

You must not accept travel or accommodation paid for by a business partner. If a business trip involves a business partner, for example a site visit or a conference they invite you to, Acme pays for your travel and accommodation. Exceptions require prior written approval from the Ethics & Compliance Office and are rarely granted.

## 6. Giving gifts and hospitality to business partners

The same limits apply when you give gifts or hospitality to private-sector business partners: USD 50 per gift, USD 150 per person for meals, and approval for entertainment as in section 5.2. Expenses must be recorded accurately in the expense system with the names and companies of all attendees.

## 7. Public officials

Bribery laws treat public officials more strictly. You must **not** give any gift, entertainment, hospitality or other benefit to a public official, except:

- Branded promotional items worth **less than USD 10** (pens, notebooks), with prior approval from the Ethics & Compliance Office; or
- Modest refreshments, such as coffee and sandwiches, during a meeting at Acme's premises.

Any other hospitality for a public official, including meals and travel for official site inspections, requires prior written approval from the Legal Department. Never give anything to a public official, or their family, while they are deciding something that affects Acme, such as a permit, inspection, tax assessment or customs clearance.

## 8. Gift Register

Record the following in the Gift Register within 5 business days: every gift received or given above USD 50, every item requiring approval under this policy, and every declined gift above USD 50. Include the date, the business partner, a description, the estimated value and the approval reference.

## 9. Questions and breaches

If you are unsure whether something is allowed, ask the Ethics & Compliance Office before accepting or giving it. Breaches of this policy may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal.
