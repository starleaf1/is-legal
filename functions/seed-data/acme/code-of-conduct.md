# Acme Corp Code of Conduct

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. Fictional.*

Version 4.0, effective 1 January 2025. Owner: Ethics & Compliance Office.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This Code sets out how everyone at Acme Corp is expected to behave. It applies to all employees, officers and directors of Acme Corp and its subsidiaries, and to contractors and temporary staff while they work for us. Where a local law sets a stricter standard than this Code, follow the law. Where this Code is stricter than the law, follow this Code.

The Code is supported by detailed policies, including the Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy (GE-01) and the Data Handling and Generative AI Tools Policy (DH-03). If a policy and this Code appear to conflict, ask the Ethics & Compliance Office.

## 2. Making the right decision

Before acting, ask yourself:

- Is it legal?
- Is it consistent with this Code and our policies?
- Would I be comfortable if it were reported in the news or explained to my manager?

If the answer to any of these is "no" or "I'm not sure", stop and ask your manager or the Ethics & Compliance Office (compliance@acme.example).

## 3. Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest arises when your personal interests, or those of a close family member, could interfere with your ability to act in Acme's best interest.

You must disclose actual or potential conflicts to your manager and to the Ethics & Compliance Office through the Disclosure Portal before acting. Examples that must be disclosed:

- Outside employment, consulting or board roles, paid or unpaid.
- A financial interest of more than 1% in a supplier, customer or competitor.
- Hiring, supervising or making procurement decisions involving a close family member or partner.
- Personal business dealings with a supplier you manage.

Disclosure is not an admission of wrongdoing. Most conflicts can be managed, for example by removing you from a decision.

## 4. Gifts, entertainment and hospitality

Gifts and hospitality can build goodwill, but they must never influence, or appear to influence, a business decision. Never offer or accept cash or cash equivalents such as gift cards. Never offer or accept anything during an active tender or contract negotiation with the other party. Follow the limits, approvals and registration rules in the Gifts, Entertainment and Hospitality Policy (GE-01).

## 5. Anti-bribery and corruption

Acme has zero tolerance for bribery. You must never offer, promise, give, request or accept anything of value to improperly influence a decision, whether the other party is a public official or a private business.

Facilitation payments, meaning small payments to speed up routine government actions such as customs clearance, permits or visas, are prohibited even where they are customary. If you are asked for one, refuse and report it to the Ethics & Compliance Office. The only exception is where you face an immediate threat to your personal safety; in that case, pay, get to safety, and report the payment within 24 hours.

Third parties acting for Acme (agents, distributors, customs brokers) must follow the same rules. Never use a third party to do something you are not allowed to do yourself.

## 6. Confidential information

Information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always at least Confidential. Share Confidential and Restricted information only with people who need it for their work and are authorized to see it. Do not discuss confidential matters in public places. Your duty of confidentiality continues after you leave Acme.

## 7. Company assets, systems and AI tools

Use company equipment, systems and accounts for legitimate business purposes. Limited personal use is acceptable if it doesn't interfere with your work or break any policy. Only use software and AI tools approved by IT, and follow the Data Handling and Generative AI Tools Policy (DH-03) on what information may be entered into them.

## 8. Social media and public statements

Only authorized spokespeople may speak on behalf of Acme. When posting personally, make clear that your views are your own, never share Internal, Confidential or Restricted information, and never comment on rumored deals, results or litigation.

## 9. Respect and fair treatment

We do not tolerate harassment, bullying or discrimination based on any protected characteristic. This applies at work, at work events and online. Report concerns to your manager, HR or the Ethics Hotline.

## 10. Speaking up

If you suspect a breach of the law, this Code or a policy, report it to your manager, HR, the Ethics & Compliance Office, or the confidential Ethics Hotline (ethics.acme.example or +99 800 555 0100), which accepts anonymous reports.

Acme prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or cooperates with an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious breach of this Code.

## 11. Consequences

Breaching this Code, a policy or the law may lead to disciplinary action up to and including dismissal, and may be reported to the authorities.
