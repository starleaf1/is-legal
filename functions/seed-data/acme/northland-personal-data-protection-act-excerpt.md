# Northland Personal Data Protection Act 2021 (Excerpt)

*Sample document for the Is Legal demo. "Northland" is a fictional country and this Act is invented.*

Enacted by the Parliament of Northland. In force from 1 January 2022. Supervised by the Northland Data Protection Authority.

Article 2 Definitions

"personal data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including names, contact details, identification numbers, location data and online identifiers.

"sensitive personal data" means personal data revealing health, biometric or genetic data, religious beliefs, political opinions, sexual orientation, criminal records, or financial account details.

"controller" means a person or organisation that decides why and how personal data is processed.

"processor" means a person or organisation that processes personal data on behalf of a controller, including providers of cloud, software and AI services.

Article 5 Lawful processing

A controller may process personal data only where the individual has given consent, or where processing is necessary to perform a contract with the individual, to comply with a legal obligation, or for the controller's legitimate interests where these are not overridden by the individual's rights.

Article 6 Purpose limitation

Personal data collected for one purpose must not be used for a different, incompatible purpose without a new lawful basis.

Article 9 Sensitive personal data

Sensitive personal data may be processed only with the individual's explicit consent or where expressly permitted by law.

Article 12 Data minimisation and retention

Controllers must process only the personal data that is necessary for the purpose and keep it no longer than necessary.

Article 18 Processors

(1) A controller may disclose personal data to a processor only under a written contract requiring the processor to process the data solely on the controller's instructions, to keep it confidential and secure, and not to use it for its own purposes, including training its own systems.

(2) Disclosing personal data to a processor without such a contract is a contravention of this Act.

Article 22 Transfers outside Northland

Personal data may be transferred outside Northland only to a country the Authority has declared adequate, or where the controller has put in place safeguards approved by the Authority, such as standard contractual clauses, and has assessed the risks of the transfer.

Article 27 Personal data breaches

(1) A controller must notify the Authority of a personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, unless the breach is unlikely to result in a risk to individuals.

(2) Where a breach is likely to result in a high risk to individuals, the controller must also inform the affected individuals without undue delay.

(3) "Personal data breach" includes accidental or unlawful disclosure of personal data to an unauthorised recipient.

Article 31 Penalties

The Authority may impose an administrative fine of up to 4% of the controller's annual worldwide turnover or NLD 20,000,000, whichever is higher, for contraventions of Articles 5, 9, 18, 22 or 27.
